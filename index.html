Tooldeck Pin Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who we are

Tooldeck is a shop on Etsy that sells digital tools: tooldeck.etsy.com. This page describes the Tooldeck Pin Publisher, an internal tool that Tooldeck uses to publish Pins to its own Pinterest business account. The tool has no other users. It is not offered or sold to anyone.

What the tool does

  • Publishes image Pins and video Pins to the Tooldeck Pinterest account. A person at Tooldeck reviews and approves every Pin before it is published.
  • Deletes Pins that Tooldeck created, for example when the Etsy listing a Pin links to is no longer available. A person at Tooldeck approves every deletion first.
  • Reads the boards, Pins and analytics of the Tooldeck Pinterest account only.

The tool does not read, collect or store information about other Pinterest users. It does not read, send or reply to messages or comments, and it does not follow accounts or save other people's Pins.

Information we keep

  • Access tokens and app credentials. The Pinterest access and refresh tokens for the Tooldeck account, and the credentials of our developer app, are stored on Tooldeck's own computer, outside any cloud-synced folder. They are not shared with anyone.
  • Publishing log. For each Pin we publish, we keep the content we sent (our own text and image), the Pin ID returned by Pinterest, its status and the time.
  • Weekly totals. We keep weekly summary numbers for our own account (for example, total Pin impressions, saves and outbound clicks) in our internal reports.

We do not keep any other information that we access through the Pinterest API.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell Pinterest information or share it with third parties, except the service providers we use to run our tools, such as the AI assistant that helps us write our weekly summary.
  • We do not use information from Pinterest for advertising.
  • We do not combine information from Pinterest with information about our Etsy buyers.
  • We do not use information from Pinterest to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

This page

This page uses no cookies, scripts, analytics or tracking.

Retention and deletion

We keep the publishing log and weekly totals for as long as we use the tool. If we stop using the tool, we revoke its access to Pinterest and delete the tokens, the app credentials, the log and the weekly totals. The account owner can also revoke the tool's access at any time in the Pinterest account settings.

Contact

For questions about this policy, send a message to the Tooldeck shop on Etsy: tooldeck.etsy.com.

Changes

If the tool changes what it does with information from Pinterest, we update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
